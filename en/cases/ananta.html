01 · The challenge

Customer service and alerts without a system of their own

Customer service ran through WhatsApp without a shared inbox. Each person saw only their part, so no one had the full conversation when they needed it.

Alerts depended on someone remembering to send them or on email threads. A missed alert poses a risk for a security company.

  • WhatsApp without a shared inbox.
  • Manual alerts with no log.
  • No database of their own to support automation.
02 · What we built

Their own inbox and automatic alerts

Chatwoot deployment

A self-hosted instance for the entire team, with the full conversation in one place.

WhatsApp connection

The channel they already used, integrated into the inbox.

Database

Supabase stores contacts, conversations, and alerts.

Notification system

Events trigger alerts automatically, so no one has to remember to send them.
