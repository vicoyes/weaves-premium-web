01 · The challenge

Every session was managed by hand

The school grew by offering courses on specific dates. Each workshop opened for registration with its own schedule, venue, and instructor. The site was set up for a fixed catalog. Whenever registration opened, the team had to edit pages, check available seats, and respond to messages one by one.

Forms had multiplied and the team followed up by email. With three courses open in three cities, the team needed to clarify who was in charge before considering another tool.

  • A rigid website for a business that changes dates every month.
  • Seats and reminders managed by hand.
  • Duplicate forms and scattered notifications.
02 · What we built

One system for all course sessions

Website by course and date

Dedicated landing pages for each workshop and city, plus a 20th anniversary page.

Spazio Booking

A custom booking plugin that manages capacity, reminders, and transactional emails, with an evergreen mode for selling courses without a set date.

Forms and CRM

Simplified forms connected to a CRM so each registration arrives with tags and its source.

Automation and email

n8n automations and email marketing campaigns use the same contact database.
