01 · The challenge

Four channels with no way to track leads

Leads came in through WhatsApp, email, social media, and the website form. Each channel ran on its own schedule. Nobody knew how long it took to respond to a lead or which leads enrolled.

Student enrollment and payment recovery depended on specific people. Contracts were signed by email, overdue installments were followed up one at a time, and there was no written protocol the team could use when someone was on vacation.

  • Channels with no SLAs or response alerts.
  • Contracts outside the enrollment workflow.
  • Payment recovery with no protocol or tracking dashboard.
02 · What we built

From lead to payment

HubSpot funnel

All four channels in one workflow, with SLAs for each channel and alerts when a lead goes cold.

Email in Mautic

Documented email sequences, with two sends completed and a third prepared.

Documenso

Contracts and signatures integrated into student enrollment, with the client's branding.

Payment recovery in Kommo

A protocol, an operations dashboard, and WhatsApp broadcasts for outstanding accounts.
