Editorial standards

What we publish
and what we leave out.

What we include
We report what we delivered, the tools we installed, operating volumes when available, and where the project stands.
What we leave out
Estimated savings, rounded percentages, filler quotes, and results without records to back them up.
When clients request privacy
We remove the client's name and identifying details. We keep the type of business, the work, and any volumes we can verify.
