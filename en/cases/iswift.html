01 · The challenge

A blog that sat apart from the rest of the site

The blog was missing from the menu, the post listing and articles had separate layouts, and comments were duplicated across templates. Every change had to be made twice.

Spam also came in through the forms. Publishing or changing a section required someone with access to the code.

  • The blog was outside the menu and shared site design.
  • Comments were duplicated across templates.
  • Publishing or making adjustments required code changes.
02 · What we built

A blog integrated with the rest of the site

Post listing and article pages

The blog home page and article pages use the same design as the rest of the site.

Comments

One component reused across the site.

Spam protection

Review of CAPTCHA and honeypots in the forms.

Assisted publishing

Claude connects to WordPress through MCP so users can work from the editor.
