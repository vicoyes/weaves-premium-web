01 · The challenge

A brand that needed a design system and two languages

Before selling anything, the brand needed a logo, a color palette, typography, and a tone for each page. Without those decisions, every new piece started from scratch.

The website had to launch in Spanish and English at the same time and lay the groundwork for a course platform to follow.

  • The identity was undefined.
  • The same content needed to work in two languages.
  • The course platform still had to be built.
02 · What we built

The design system came first, then the pages

Identity

A minimalist logo and a warm palette in paper, calypso, and sand tones.

Design system

Documented design tokens and typography rules to guide the whole product.

Bilingual website

Nine pages in Spanish and English using the same structure.

Course platform

MVP in development, with a product brief, architecture, components, and backend.
