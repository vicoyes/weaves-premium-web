Personal data we collect

We may process the following categories of data:

  • Identification data: first name, last name

We do not collect sensitive data.

Purposes of processing

We use your data to:

  • Handle the free AI assessment you request.
  • Send you the assessment results and recommendations.
  • Respond to requests and inquiries submitted through the Website.
  • Provide consulting services if you decide to engage us.
  • Send communications related to automation and AI (only if you consent).
  • Improve the Website and analyze its performance.

Legal basis

The legal bases for processing are:

  • Your consent when you complete forms or accept cookies.
  • Precontractual measures when you request an assessment or evaluation.
  • Legitimate interest in improving our services and the Website.
  • Compliance with legal obligations if you engage our services (billing, taxation, etc.).

AI assessment

If you use the assessment service:

  • Your responses are analyzed using artificial intelligence.
  • The AI automatically generates an initial report.
  • A human expert reviews your case before contacting you.
  • Your data is not used to train external AI models.

Data retention

  • Forms / assessments: for as long as needed to handle them
  • Commercial data: until you request its deletion
  • Contractual/tax information: for as long as legally required
  • Cookies: according to your browser settings

Recipients

Your data is not sold or transferred. The following providers are involved in delivering the service, each under its own data processing agreement:

  • Competent authorities when legally required.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and, where the provider participates, by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Data is not sold to third parties.
