Cookie policy

This site does not use cookies: no analytics, advertising, or social media cookies. We measure activity with Umami, an open-source tool installed on our own server that counts visits and actions without cookies, without personal identifiers, and without sending anything to third parties. There is no Google Analytics and there are no Meta or LinkedIn pixels. The only thing stored on your device is the decision you make in the cookie notice, so we do not have to ask you again on every page.

What is stored and why

  • cookieyes-consent · Records whether you accepted or rejected the notice and the date of that decision. The consent tool (CookieYes) sets it. Duration: 1 year. It does not identify anyone and is not shared with third parties.
  • Browser preferences · The site records in local storage whether you have already seen certain entrance animations so that it does not replay them. This data stays on your device.

External resources that receive your IP address

Even though these are not cookies, when the page loads, your browser requests two things from third-party servers, and your IP address is sent with those requests:

  • Google Fonts · The site's fonts are downloaded from Google.
  • Cloudflare · The site is served through its network, which receives the connection before it reaches the server.

We are moving the fonts to our own server to eliminate that request to Google. We will update this page once the move is complete.

What we measure

We measure which pages are visited, where visits come from (a search engine, link, or campaign), and specific actions such as viewing a service, opening a case study, clicking a call to action, or submitting the form. This tells us which content is useful. Your IP address is not stored in the database or matched with personal data, and we do not use advertising or profiling.

How to change your decision

You can revisit your choice at any time using the “Configure cookies” link in the notice or by clearing site data in your browser settings (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). If you reject the notice, the site works the same way. No content or features depend on those cookies.
