01 · CUSTOMER SERVICE AND COMMUNICATION
Customer service and communication
For teams that receive inquiries across several channels and spend time sorting, responding, or looking for context.
  • Sort WhatsApp, email, and web messages
  • Answer approved frequently asked questions
  • Request information or documents before routing
  • Escalate urgent or sensitive cases to the team
See managed customer service
02 · GROWTH AND FOLLOW-UP
Growth and follow-up
For businesses that generate contacts but miss opportunities because recording, prioritizing, or following up depends on manual tasks.
  • Capture contacts from forms and WhatsApp
  • Classify opportunities and update the CRM
  • Trigger reminders and follow-up sequences
  • Prepare campaigns and notify sales when they need to step in
See sales follow-up
03 · ADMINISTRATION AND DOCUMENTS
Administration and documents
For teams that spend hours reading emails, moving data, preparing documents, or updating different systems.
  • Read inboxes, attachments, and forms

  • Update systems and generate documents
  • Prepare reports and flag errors
